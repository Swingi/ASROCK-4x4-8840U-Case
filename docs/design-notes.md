# Design notes

## Cooling direction

The enclosure is intended to support a large filtered intake and a separate exhaust path. The goal is to move cool air across the original CPU heatsink while also providing airflow around SSD, VRM and RAM areas.

## Current geometric baseline

- Base: 170 x 170 x 10 mm
- Foot height: 40 mm
- Ventilation field: 150 x 150 mm
- Intake target: 140 mm fan
- Exhaust target: 120 mm fan
- Fasteners: M3

These are starting parameters, not final manufacturing dimensions. Board mounting points, fan-hole patterns and the original heatsink geometry should be measured before final printing.
